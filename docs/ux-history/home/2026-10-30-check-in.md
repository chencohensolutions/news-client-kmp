# Home — Hebrew chrome, check-in, stories first (2026-10-30)

Tag: `ux/home/2026-10-30`

The round covered the whole headline screen (the three views as one screen), on the owner's request to read the app
and suggest the best UX for his needs: many short visits a day, in Hebrew, to see what changed and compare sites.

## Options
- **a — ליטוש (polish)**: the current structure in Hebrew, right-to-left, with icons; view tabs pinned under the
  title; a chip per site; one banner per failed site; stories in cards. *Turned down*: superseded by b.
- **b — צ׳ק-אין (check-in)**: views in a bottom bar; "עודכן לפני 4 דק׳ · 8 חדשות" under the title; new headlines
  marked with an edge bar and first in אחרונות above a "מהביקור הקודם" divider; counted per category, badged on a
  story; site chips pinned with a warning on a failed site; tinted site labels. *Liked*, revised into b2.
- **b-color**: b in Material You colors. *Liked*, folded into b2.
- **b2**: b after the owner's review: Stories is the opening view; the stories feed is a flat list with dividers
  (the lead's picture across the width at its own ratio, the lead title in large type, site and time on every
  report); category chips under the site chips follow the scroll and jump; Material You colors; arrivals animate
  into place (at the top the list follows them up; elsewhere a "N סיפורים חדשים" pill offers the way up). *Picked*,
  promoted in a7f46f0.
- **c — סריקה (scan)**: text only, two lines a headline; אחרונות grouped by hour; category chips that jump; stories
  collapsed to lines; the site filter as a menu in the title bar. *Turned down*: the owner preferred b's pictures;
  its category chips live on in b2.

The owner's words on b: Stories should be the default screen; the bottom navigation stays; no cards in the stories
feed, a flat list with dividers; time on the related articles; a story item can be larger, with the image in its
real aspect ratio; in categories, a way to pick a category. Later: stories take long to load on app start (cache
smarter) and an incoming article should emerge gracefully.

## What they faked
- b, b2: `CheckInState` / `CheckInStateB2` (when the feed was fetched; which headlines are new since the owner last
  had the list open). The view model has neither; FeedCache would save the fetch time with the headlines, and the
  view model would remember the feed's URLs when the app leaves the foreground.
- b2: Stories as the opening view (AppSettings.startView defaults to CATEGORIES today). Three story reports had no
  time on their list pages and were given one, as "השלמה מעמודי הכתבות" would. A pull fakes a refresh that brings one
  story in after 1.5 s.
- b, b2, c: tinted site labels from a map of source id to hue in the screen (no schema change).
- All: the Material icon set (`material-icons-extended`) is a lab-only dependency until a screen is promoted.
