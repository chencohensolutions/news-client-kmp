# Home — collapsed site filter, hide read, order (2026-10-30)

Tag: `ux/home/2026-10-30-3`

With 26 sites, the row of site chips under the title had grown too long to use. The owner asked for something that
collapses, and in the same round for a way to hide what was already read; after picking c, for ordering by time or by
the number of related articles.

## Options
- **a — תפריט (menu)**: "כל האתרים ▾" opens a menu of the sites (a check on the chosen one, a warning on a failing
  one), beside a "בלי נקראו" chip. *Not picked*.
- **b — נפתח (fold-out)**: the same button unfolds every site as chips that wrap onto a few lines, folding again once
  one is picked; "בלי נקראו" beside it. *Not picked*.
- **c — סינון (filter sheet)**: no row under the title; a filter button in the title bar (a dot while filtered) opens
  a sheet with "הסתרת כתבות שנקראו" and every site; chips with an ✕ under the title show the active filters.
  *Picked*, revised into c2.
- **c2**: c with "מיון" in the sheet: "לפי זמן" (newest first) or "לפי כתבות קשורות" (stories with the most reports
  first; in אחרונות, headlines by the size of their story); a new order starts at the top. The owner turned down a
  time window ("no filter by time, sort by time"). *Picked*, promoted in 67b1b4d.

## What they faked
- a, b, c, c2: hide read. The lab filtered the sample feed (a headline once read, a story once any of its reports was
  read); no state or setting holds it.
- c2: the order (`FeedSortC2`), held in the lab; the screen sorts what the state gives it.
