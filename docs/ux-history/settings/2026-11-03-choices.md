# Settings — the rows of choices (2026-11-03, Android)

Tag: `ux/settings/2026-11-03`

Settings' choices were rows of segmented buttons, up to six squeezed into the screen's width ("רענון בחזרה
לאפליקציה", "תדירות", "טווח הסיפורים", "מקום לתמונות"): "too crowded, find better UX". The owner added: amounts
like days or hours could be sliders (option d).

## Options
- **a — ערך וגיליון (value and sheet)**: a choice is one row, its title, its explanation (two lines at most) and the
  value chosen at its end in the accent color with a ›; a tap opens a bottom sheet with the whole explanation and
  every choice on its own line with a radio button. *Revised* into a2, a3, a4.
- **b — תפריט (menu)**: the value chosen in a small rounded button at the row's end with a ▾; a tap drops a menu
  down in place, a check by the chosen one. *Not picked*.
- **c — צ׳יפים (chips)**: the choices stay one tap away as roomy chips on one line that scrolls sideways, starting at
  the chosen one. *Not picked*.
- **d — מחוון לכמויות (sliders for amounts)**: every amount (hours, days, minutes, how many, room, the wait between a
  site's articles) is a slider whose stops are its choices, the value in large type beside the title and the two ends
  named under it, as the stories' strictness is; "off" at the low end, "all" or "no limit" at the high end. The other
  choices (the model, what stories compare) are a's value-and-sheet row. *Not picked*.
- The owner leaned to a ("ערך וגיליון") but: its chevron pointed backward for RTL, it shouldn't be blue, and a
  chevron suggests another screen opens. Apple keeps the chevron for going to another screen and marks a one-of-a-list
  pop-up button with ↕; Android's own settings show the value under the title with no mark (a ListPreference's
  summary). So:
- **a2 — ערך וחץ מתוקן**: a with the chevron pointing forward for RTL (‹), grey; the value in the text's color. *Not picked*.
- **a3 — ערך וסימן בחירה ↕**: a with a grey up-and-down chooser mark instead of the chevron. *Picked*, promoted in 857b75e.
- **a4 — הערך מתחת לכותרת**: no mark; the value is a line under the title, as Android's settings show it. *Not picked*.

## What they faked
- Nothing in the state: each option is a stand-in page of Settings' choice rows only, in their sections with the
  app's texts (the switches, model row, sliders and pages left out), and the choices work.
